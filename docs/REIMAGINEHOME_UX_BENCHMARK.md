# Photo entry and guided design

The public ReimagineHome homepage and anonymous app entry were inspected on 22 November 2026. The app offered a device-upload/drop area and example photos covering furnished rooms, vacant rooms, exteriors and plans. Its public presentation illustrated choosing a goal, optional references and comparing an original against the result. Inspection ended at the entry screen. [Marketing reference](https://www.reimaginehome.ai/) · [Public app](https://app.reimaginehome.ai/en)

| Observed task | Vastu Nord implementation |
|---|---|
| Start with a photo | The hero opens Room Reimagination at its source-image section. It offers a private upload where the authenticated application enables it. |
| Explore with a sample | The second action opens the selected, allowlisted brand study in the editor. The choice survives reload and tool navigation. |
| Decide what to change | The existing transformation chooser and 17-tool library lead to the documented tool-specific settings. |
| Inspect the difference | A keyboard- and touch-operable comparison shows the existing original and its illustrative evening treatment. Generated output requires a verified provider workflow in a later phase. |

The approved Figma boards, brand guide, original Studio and rights-reviewed Vastu Nord artwork govern the interface and its copy. [Figma mapping](FIGMA_TO_ROUTE_MATRIX.md) · [Studio configuration](STUDIO_TOOL_CONFIGURATION_MATRIX.md)
