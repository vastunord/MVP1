## Phase 01E-A outcome

Desktop marketing route inspection for the previously identified routes is complete, including six branded 404 responses. Phase 01E-B observed the workspace and four representative category editors at the available desktop viewport. This is not an assertion of pixel-perfect parity. Requested 1440px, 768px and 390px states could not be set; touch behavior and durable screenshots remain open gaps. AI, billing, storage, search results, account behavior, hidden states and source implementations remain unverified. Overall Phase 01 UI-parity remains BLOCKED; Phase 01E-C and Phase 02 have not started.
